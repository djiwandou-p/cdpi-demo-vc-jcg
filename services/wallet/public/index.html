walt.id Verifiable Credential Demo

Issue → store → present an Education Certificate with selective disclosure.

1. Hold a credential

Paste a credential offer (an openid-credential-offer:// URI or offer JSON), or scan a QR code. Then accept the offer to run the OpenID4VCI exchange and store the credential.

Paste the offer string, or use “Scan QR” to decode it from a camera or image.

2. Present a credential

Paste a presentation request (an openid4vp:// URI or request JSON), or scan a QR code. The wallet reveals only degree and institution and withholds grades.

Paste the request string, or use “Scan QR” to decode it from a camera or image.
